# The T-House implementation status

## Rebuild scope

The full website follows [Website Master Plan v1.1](MASTER-PLAN.md). Its routes cover Home, Watch, the individual release, Inside the House, About and Connect. The homepage feature opens the episode page, where viewers can load the privacy-enhanced player, share the episode or watch directly on YouTube. Its official source supplies the title, 3:43 duration, publication timestamp and thumbnail. [Official upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9adqs1vgcMA)

| Area | Implementation and review |
| --- | --- |
| Site and motion | House-inspired entrance, integrated Warp Carousel with four destination links, progressively enhanced scroll motion, accessible menu and reduced-motion control. |
| Real content | A typed record for the one verified public upload. The library can grow as more releases are verified. |
| Studio identity | The supplied mark informs the white house elevation, red brush script, charcoal surfaces and architectural framing across the site. A higher-resolution logo master, approved studio photography and definitive profile copy remain open. |
| Contact | The Connect collage leads to the official Instagram and YouTube links. Visitors can copy the studio handle or draft and copy an enquiry for Instagram. The route also shows the verified episode and a short FAQ. Direct form delivery, file handling, public visit details, bookings and audio playback need the sources listed in the [Connect page plan](CONNECT-PAGE-PLAN.md). |
| Editorial management | Repository-based updates. A managed CMS depends on the editorial owner, account, draft process and preferences. |
| Hosting and measurement | The full site is live on its Netlify subdomain. A live check passed for all six pages, the unknown-episode 404, local and YouTube image delivery, FAQ search and the Instagram draft. The deployed pages carry noindex. Custom domain, search indexing, Search Console, GA4 properties and consent decisions remain open. [Live website](https://the-t-house.netlify.app/) · [Netlify deploy](https://app.netlify.com/projects/the-t-house/deploys/6afa11825f82d2bf149b6e11) |
