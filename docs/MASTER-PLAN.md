# The T-House website master plan

The Website Master Plan v1.1, dated 14 November 2026, governs the rebuild. The user asked to move from the previous motion prototype to the full website. The site helps visitors find and share The T-House’s content, follow its channels and learn about the studio in Leyton, East London.

The illuminated architectural outline carries the experience from the first view to the latest video. Fabian Tjoe-A-On’s design informs the layers of depth, lighting around content and continuity between views. The T-House interpretation uses its own house motif and warm tones. [Design reference](https://fabiantjoeaon.com/) · [Recorded walkthrough and screenshots](https://www.landing.love/sites/fabiantjoeaon/)

## Website

| Route | Visitor journey |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Enter the house, reach the latest video or studio immediately and find the official social channels. The house motif leads into the featured content as visitors scroll. |
| `/watch` | Discover the verified public video and browse additional releases as they are published. |
| `/watch/[slug]` | Open a shareable video page, choose the embedded player or YouTube, and find relevant links. |
| `/inside-the-house` | Learn about the studio and see the visual concept for the studio section. Approved photos can replace the current artwork. |
| `/about` | Read the studio’s verified location, creative context and association with SV on the Beat. |
| `/connect` | Reach the studio through its official social profiles. |

The channel’s Videos page listed one public upload when verified on 14 November 2026. The website features that real upload. Library filters, related content, guest profiles and transcripts depend on available records and permissions. [Official Videos page](https://www.youtube.com/@thethousestudio/videos) · [Verified release](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9adqs1vgcMA)

## Review and launch

- Obtain approved logo assets, studio photographs and image rights. Confirm approved copy for SV’s profile, production credits and the studio story.
- Confirm who owns content review and publication. Select an editor interface after the editorial workflow and account access are agreed.
- Confirm the production origin, hosting and permission to index or launch. Set up analytics and audience baselines once account access and consent requirements are known.
- Confirm the contact destination and consent for handling enquiries before introducing a form. The current Connect page points to the official channels.
- Verify direct routes, keyboard and touch navigation, reduced motion, media fallbacks, sharing and accessibility on the integrated site. Repeat deployment checks on the approved host.
